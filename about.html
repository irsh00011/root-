About Us

Handmade, from our shop
to your home

Roots Handicraft is a handicraft shop in Vandalur, Chennai — an arts & crafts store for handmade gifts, home decor and souvenirs.

Every piece here is made to be looked at, gifted, and kept at home — from dancing Tanjore dolls and wooden pieces to small figurines and decor that adds colour to a room.

Come and see the pieces in the shop, or ask us on WhatsApp about any piece you liked on this site.
